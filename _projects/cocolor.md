CoColor is an interactive system for exploring AI-assisted color design workflows.

I contributed to the literature review, interaction design, implementation, and user testing with professional UX designers. The work was published at ACM IUI 2023.

[Paper](https://dl.acm.org/doi/abs/10.1145/3581641.3584089)
